Picker — View State & Format Bug Tests

Covers the two fixes in @strata-packages/picker@1.0.3: stale calendar view state on reopen, and date-format token substring collision (MMM/MMMM).

Test 1 — Calendar opens at the right month not tested

Bug: navigating the calendar without selecting a date left vY/vM at that stale month. Every subsequent open (even after a hard refresh) jumped to that month.
Expected: empty input → always opens at today. Filled input → opens at the selected date.

  1. Click the empty input below — verify it opens on today.
  2. Navigate a few months back or forward (do not pick a date), then close.
  3. Click the input again — it must reopen on today, not the month you navigated to.
  4. Now pick a date. Close. Reopen — calendar must show the selected date's month.
  5. Clear the input. Reopen — back to today.

Expected: clicking June 2026 → opens month grid. Clicking the year in month grid → opens year grid. Clicking a year → back to month grid. Clicking a month → back to day+time view.

  1. Open the picker below. You should see the day grid with June 2026 (or current month) as the title.
  2. Click the title button (e.g. "June 2026") — a 12-month grid must appear.
  3. Click the year shown in the month grid header — a 12-year grid must appear.
  4. Click any year → back to month grid. Click any month → back to day+time view.
  5. Confirm the time columns and Apply/Clear buttons return correctly when back on day view.
