Breakpoint-Scoped Arbitrary Values

Resize your browser to see each utility change at its breakpoint.

xs <576px · sm ≥576px · md ≥768px · lg ≥992px · xl ≥1200px · xxl ≥1400px

1 — px-sm-[var(--space-40)]

padding-left/right jump to a custom token (2.5rem) at sm+ — no fixed-scale step matches this value

Box with token-driven horizontal padding

↑ Small padding on mobile → 2.5rem left/right padding at ≥576px

2 — p-md-[1rem_2rem]

padding shorthand (1rem 2rem) at md+ — underscore-to-space unescaping

Box with shorthand padding at md+

↑ Uniform padding on mobile → asymmetric 1rem/2rem padding at ≥768px

3 — !m-lg-[3rem]

margin forced with !important at lg+

Box with forced margin at lg+

↑ Small margin on mobile → 3rem margin (!important) at ≥992px

4 — border-sm-[3px_dashed_var(--st-primary)]

border shorthand with a custom width/style/color combo at sm+

Box with dashed border at md+

↑ No border on mobile → 4px dashed danger-colored border at ≥768px

5 — !border-xl-[2px_solid_var(--st-success)]

forced border at xl+

Box with forced success border at xl+

↑ Default 1px border on mobile → forced 2px solid success border at ≥1200px

6 — shadow-lg-[var(--st-shadow-custom)]

box-shadow using a project-specific token that isn't on Strata's 3-step scale, gated to lg+

Box with custom shadow token at lg+

↑ No shadow on mobile → custom purple shadow at ≥992px

7 — !shadow-xxl-[0_20px_40px_rgba(0,0,0,0.25)]

forced box-shadow with a raw multi-token value at xxl+

Box with forced deep shadow at xxl+

↑ Small shadow by default → forced deep shadow (!important) at ≥1400px

8 — combined px / border / shadow at md+

Realistic case: a card that gets breathing room, a border, and a shadow together once there's room

Card that gains padding, border, and shadow at md+

↑ Bare box on mobile → padded, bordered, shadowed card at ≥768px

9 — border-x / border-y (new)

border-x sets left+right in one class; border-y sets top+bottom — didn't exist before this pass

border-x — left/right border only
border-y — top/bottom border only

↑ No breakpoint needed to see these — always-on combined-side borders

10 — border-top-md / border-top-md-0

Fixed 1px side border that only appears at md+, and its removal counterpart

border-top-md — top border appears at ≥768px
border-x-lg — left/right borders appear at ≥992px

↑ Resize to see side borders switch on at their breakpoint

11 — border-top-[...] / border-x-md-[...]

Custom per-side border value, optionally gated to a breakpoint

border-top-[3px_dashed_var(--st-danger)] — always on
!border-x-md-[2px_solid_var(--st-success)] — forced at ≥768px

↑ Dashed top border always visible → forced green side borders appear at ≥768px

12 — rounded-top-lg (new)

Only the top corners round off, and only at lg+ — corner-pair classes had no bp variant before

rounded-top-lg — top corners round at ≥992px

↑ Square corners on mobile → rounded top corners at ≥992px

13 — rounded-top-[...] / rounded-top-md-[...]

Custom radius value applied to both top corners, optionally gated to a breakpoint

rounded-top-[12px_4px] — always on
rounded-top-md-[20px] — forced at ≥768px

↑ Custom elliptical top-corner radius → larger radius appears at ≥768px

14 — rounded-[...] / rounded-md-[...] (new — full corner arbitrary didn't exist)

A project-specific radius token that isn't on Strata's 0-5/pill/circle scale

rounded-[var(--space-40)] — always on
!rounded-xl-[50%] — forced circular at ≥1200px

↑ Token-driven radius always on → forced fully-round shape at ≥1200px

15 — outline-md-primary / outline-[...] / outline-md-[...] (new)

Outline family had zero breakpoint variants and no arbitrary form at all before this pass

outline-md-primary — named outline appears at ≥768px
outline-[2px_dashed_var(--st-danger)] — custom outline, always on
!outline-lg-[3px_solid_var(--st-success)] — forced custom outline at ≥992px

↑ Dashed outline always visible → named outline at ≥768px, forced solid outline at ≥992px

16 — g-[...] / gx-md-[...] / gy-lg-[...] (new)

Gutters already had a responsive named scale (0-5) but no arbitrary form for custom values

g-[var(--space-40)] col A
g-[var(--space-40)] col B
gx-md-[3rem] col A
gx-md-[3rem] col B

↑ Token-driven gutter always on → wide 3rem horizontal gutter appears at ≥768px
